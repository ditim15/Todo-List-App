Scope: the whole DoneYet? client (landing, auth, dashboard/tasks, notes, account, privacy, contact). Mode: Operate for the app screens; the landing page (Splash) is Persuade inside the same world.

Audience and job: students (and anyone) capturing tasks on a phone between commitments and planning or writing notes on a laptop. Constraints: works 320px to wide desktop, no horizontal scroll, 44px targets, mobile nav and notes editor usable, light and dark themes. Name "DoneYet?", the hand-drawn strike and green are brand commitments.

## Direction contract

THESIS: DoneYet? is a crisp printed handout you mark up: a green highlighter swipe says "this next", a biro line crosses off what's done. It refuses the cream-paper planner and the grey SaaS sidebar app.

OWN-WORLD: bright white stock (light) or a lamp-lit black sheet (dark); ink-black text; 2px ink rules and hairline dividers instead of shadows and cards; near-square corners; one heavy grotesque (Archivo) for display and UI; fluorescent highlighter green as the marking colour, brand ink green for actions; biro blue only for hand marks (strike, tick).

STORY: the visitor sees today's work set like a handout, sees what's next because it is highlighted, adds a task in one line, ticks it and watches the biro strike land.

FIRST VIEWPORT: slim top bar ruled by a 2px ink line (logo, Tasks/Notes, theme, account; collapses to a bottom-safe menu on phones). Dashboard: big tight heading with a highlighted count ("3 things today"), a single-line quick add under it, then Today and Coming up as ruled rows; lists in a left column on desktop, a horizontal scroller on phones. Landing: the same handout headline with the highlighter swipe over the promise, primary CTA beside it.

FORM: Highlighted Handout, my top-ranked grounded candidate (position 1), chosen by the user as IMPECCABLE'S PICK; seed:6ec54d36661b76ad.

Signature move: the highlighter swipe (a skewed, slightly ragged fluorescent band behind text) marks next-up items, active nav and the landing promise; the biro strike draws across finished tasks.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
